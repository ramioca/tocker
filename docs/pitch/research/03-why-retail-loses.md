## Why retail loses: no edge, rugs, bots, no exits

*Note: WebFetch was blocked, so figures come from search-indexed text of the cited pages. Check the primary wording before print.*

### Headline stats (pitch-ready). The best stat for each reason is marked ★
- ★ **No alpha: about 21,000 new pump.fun tokens a day, one every 4 seconds.** CoinGecko Research, Jun 2026: 18.67M tokens between 14 Jan 2024 and 18 Jun 2026 ([CryptoPotato](https://cryptopotato.com/nearly-70-of-pump-fun-tokens-die-on-launch-day-coingecko/)). Average = 18.67M ÷ 886 days (my math). · med-high
- **Under 1% of day traders reliably beat the market after fees.** Barber, Lee, Liu & Odean, *J. Fin. Markets* 2014, Taiwan 1992–2006 ([paper](https://faculty.haas.berkeley.edu/odean/papers/day%20traders/Day%20Trading%20Skill%20110523.pdf)) · high
- ★ **Rugged: about 7 in 10 pump.fun tokens stop trading on launch day.** CoinGecko, Jun 2026: 68.67%; only 4.55% still trade after 90 days ([crypto.news](https://crypto.news/pump-funs-token-factory-has-a-69-launch-day-death-rate-coingecko/)) · med-high
- ★ **Bots: 15,000+ pump.fun launches a month sniped in their first block.** Pine Analytics, 2025 (exact date unverified). Buyers were deployer-funded; 87% of snipes were profitable ([Substack](https://pineanalytics.substack.com/p/exit-liquidity-machines)). A Solana slot is ~400 ms ([docs](https://solana.com/docs/references/terminology)). · med
- **Sandwich bots took $370M–$500M from Solana traders in 16 months.** sandwiched.me, presented in May 2025 ([Solana Compass](https://solanacompass.com/learn/accelerate-25/scale-or-die-at-accelerate-2025-the-state-of-solana-mev)) · med
- ★ **FOMO: the same traders buy stock dips but chase crypto pumps.** Kogan, Makarov, Niessner & Schoar, *JFE* 2024, about 200,000 eToro traders ([NBER](https://www.nber.org/papers/w31317)) · high
- **97% of Brazilians who day-traded for 300+ days lost money.** Chague, De-Losso & Giovannetti, 2019/2020, 19,646 traders ([SSRN](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=3423101)) · high
- **Only 0.4% of pump.fun wallets have ever banked $10K+ profit.** Dune data via Decrypt, around Jan 2025: 55,296 of 13.55M wallets ([Decrypt](https://decrypt.co/300403/pump-fun-traders-millionaires)) · med-high
- **In June 2025, only 30% of pump.fun traders were profitable.** CoinGecko Research, 2026 ([report](https://www.coingecko.com/research/publications/pump-fun-traders-are-making-a-comeback)) · med-high

### Supporting detail
- **BIS Bulletin 69 (20 Feb 2023).** Most crypto-app users in nearly every economy lost money on bitcoin between 2015 and 2022. After Terra and FTX collapsed, whales sold while small retail investors bought ([BIS](https://www.bis.org/publ/bisbull69.htm)). BIS Working Paper 1049 (Nov 2022) put the share at 73–81% of users, assuming $100 monthly buys ([The Register](https://www.theregister.com/2022/11/16/bitcoin_investors_lose/)).
- **No exits.** Bitcoin traders sell winners early and hold losers (the disposition effect), strongly from 2017 on (Schatzmann & Haslhofer, arXiv 2010.12415, 2020). The heaviest-trading households earned 11.4% a year against the market's 17.9% (Barber & Odean, *JF* 2000).
- **CFDs.** ESMA (Mar 2018): 74–89% of retail accounts lose money ([ESMA](https://www.esma.europa.eu/node/84933)).
- **Early-buyer rings.** arXiv 2607.02795 (Jul 2026) found 1,012 wallet rings repeatedly among the first buyers across 166,098 launches.
- **Peak volume.** Solana minted a record 263,000+ tokens in one day, 34,184 of them on pump.fun (Cointelegraph, 10 Sep 2026).
- **Math.** Checking one day's launches at 30 seconds each takes 176 hours. One minute spent on a single token is 150 Solana blocks.

### Suggested slide copy
1. **No edge.** Too many launches and too little data to judge.
2. **Rugged.** Most new tokens die the day they launch.
3. **Outrun.** Bots buy within one 0.4-second block; humans take minutes.
4. **No exits.** They chase pumps and ride losers down.

Headlines: "Retail isn't unlucky. It's outgunned." · "No edge. Rugged. Outrun. No exits."

Spoken line: "Pump.fun launches a token every four seconds, insiders snipe thousands in the first block, seven in ten die the same day, and the humans chasing them buy the pump and hold the dump. Fewer than 1 in 200 wallets ever bank $10,000."

### Caveats / what not to claim
- **The data does not support "90%+ lose on pump.fun."** CoinGecko: 50–70% were unprofitable in most months of 2024–25, but 73% were *profitable* in Apr 2026 after losers left (65% made only $1–$500). Say "most lose" or "0.4% ever bank $10K." Use 90%+ only for the day-trading data.
- 0.4% means realized profit above $10K, not that 99.6% lost. A wallet is not a person.
- Telegram "bots" such as Trojan and Axiom are human-driven. Don't use the unsourced claim that bots are 62% of Solana DEX volume.
- Sandwich losses date from 2024–25, and a 2025 crackdown reportedly cut sandwich profits by 60–70%. Use the past tense.
- Tocker checks exits every 5 minutes, so it does not win the speed race. Pitch it as watching every launch and refusing the sniped ones.
- I found no figures for how many tokens launch on Base.
