### TAM / SAM / SOM (with math)
**TAM: what long-tail traders pay to trade.**
- Solana 2025, sourced: $940M + $762M = **$1.70B**.
- All chains (estimate): Telegram-bot and trading-app fees ran ~$30–50M a week after a ~$100M peak week (to 2 Feb 2025) ([DefiLlama](https://defillama.com/pro/tg-bots-trading-apps-7y0m8c), via Step Data · low-med). $40M × 52 ≈ $2.1B, + $0.76B launchpads ≈ **~$2.8B**.

**SAM: Solana + Base trading tools** (launchpads excluded, since Tocker doesn't launch tokens).
- $940M on Solana (sourced) plus up to 10% for Base (estimate; no published figure) ≈ **$1.0B in 2025**. Base DEX: ~$34.5B per 30 days ([DefiLlama](https://defillama.com/dexs/chain/Base), 2026 · low); memecoin share unpublished.
- 2026 pace (estimate): Solana app revenue was $257M in Q2 2026, 43% of 2025's quarterly average ($2.39B ÷ 4 = $598M), so SAM ≈ **$0.43B**. August 2026 ($143M, [Crypto Briefing](https://cryptobriefing.com/solana-august-app-revenue-143m/)) implies ~$0.7B.
- Cross-check: $940M ÷ 0.9–4.8M daily trader wallets ≈ **$200–1,050 per wallet per year**.

**SOM: Tocker in year 3 at $0.10 per fill (all figures are assumptions).**
- Default agent: $100 per trade, ≤10 trades a day (≤20 fills with exits). Assume 4 fills a day: $0.40/day = **$146 a year** (max $730), on $146k of volume.
- 5k / 25k / 100k live agents → **$0.7M / $3.7M / $14.6M** in gross fees. That is 0.7% / 3.4% / 13.5% of 2025 Solana terminal volume, and 0.07% / 0.37% / 1.5% of SAM.
- Tocker's 0.10% take against terminals' ~0.87% (~9× cheaper) is both the pitch and the revenue cap.

### Caveats / what not to claim
- Don't present market cap as market size. It has run ~$35–50B through 2026 ([MEXC](https://www.mexc.com/news/714646), secondary); check [CoinGecko](https://www.coingecko.com/en/categories/meme-token) live before the pitch.
- Say "in 2025", not "per year". 2025 was the peak; in Q2 2026 Solana apps ran at 43% of that pace and pump.fun at 28%.
- Trading moves between chains: on 2 Sep 2026, 81% of terminal volume was on Robinhood Chain, outside Tocker's scope.
- Wallets are not people: weekly "traders" ran 10–15M against 0.9M daily ([Cryptonews](https://cryptonews.com/news/solana-trader-count-plummets-81-as-meme-coin-craze-turns-toxic/), Aug 2025). Never say "8M traders". I found no reliable monthly-active figure.
- Base launchpads have no comparable annual fee figure (Zora issued >50k tokens a day in Jul 2025, per [ForkLog](https://forklog.com/en/news/zora-on-base-surpasses-pump-fun-and-letsbonk-in-meme-coin-issuance)), so keep Base off the slide.
- Drop the blog claim of "BullX $2.29B fees"; [DL News](https://www.dlnews.com/articles/defi/telegram-bots-gain-in-popularity-with-more-memecoin-trading/) puts BullX at ~$200M lifetime. Axiom's lifetime revenue figures conflict ($390M vs $453M).
- CoinGecko's "$20.7B → $3.2B" is daily memecoin volume, not annual, and I found no annual CEX figure. pump.fun's $664M overlaps the launchpad line, so don't add the two.
- SOM is gross: Tocker pays for x402 data, at $0.01–0.05 per call. At 96 runs a day, data spend has to stay at or under $0.004 per run to break even at 4 fills a day ($0.021 at 20 fills).
