## Retail P&L on pump.fun (problem-slide anchor)

*Method note: WebFetch was blocked and the search budget ran out, so these figures come from search extracts of the cited articles. I didn't open the Dune, CoinGecko or Solidus sources. Spot-check the anchor before the deck ships.*

### Headline stats (pitch-ready)
- **ANCHOR: ~9 in 10 pump.fun traders lost money or made under $100.** Source: Dune wallet-PnL breakdown, Aug 18 2024. 60% lost money, 4.7% made $0 and 24% made <$100 (total 88.7%). 3% made >$1,000 and 0.5% made >$10,000 ([X post](https://x.com/crypto__kermit/status/1825109763966787894); [The Defiant](https://thedefiant.io/news/defi/just-3-of-pump-fun-traders-have-made-over-usd1000)). Independent check: [crypto.news Research, Aug 26 2024](https://crypto.news/only-0-76-of-pump-fun-wallets-made-1000-or-more-cn-research/) put the odds of a $100+ profit at 6.47% (so ~93.5% didn't make $100+). Only 0.76% made ≥$1,000. · confidence **med-high** (2024 snapshot)
- **99.6% of 13.55M pump.fun wallets never realized $10K profit.** Source: Dune (analyst Adam Tehc), Jan 2025. ~55,296 wallets realized >$10K and 293 realized >$1M ([Cointelegraph](https://cointelegraph.com/news/pump-fun-crypto-traders-majority-do-not-realize-profits-dune-data)). [Decrypt](https://decrypt.co/300403/pump-fun-traders-millionaires) reports 55,012 of 13.4M and 294. · **high**
- **60%+ of active pump.fun traders lost money.** Source: Dune, Jul 2025. Sample: 4.257M wallets that traded >10 tokens in the prior 6 months. 56.6% lost $0–$1K, ~1,700 lost >$100K and 311 made >$1M ([crypto.news](https://crypto.news/over-60-pump-fun-traders-saw-losses-less-than-0-01-made-over-1m/); [BeInCrypto](https://beincrypto.com/pump-fun-trading-data-majority-lose-money/)). · **med**
- **June 2025: only 30% of pump.fun traders were profitable.** Source: CoinGecko Research, May 2026. From Apr 2024 to late 2025, the profitable share "rarely exceed[ed] 50%" ([report](https://www.coingecko.com/research/publications/pump-fun-traders-are-making-a-comeback)). · **high**
- **Graduation: fewer than 1 in 100 tokens ever reach a DEX.** The June 2026 average was ≈0.8%. I derived this from The Block, which says a 6.7% day in late July was "roughly 8x" the June average ([The Block, Jul 29 2026](https://www.theblock.co/post/409815/pump-fun-token-graduation-rate-jumps-boost-changes-launch-incentives)). Supporting figure from Solidus Labs (May 2025): of 7M+ tokens launched by Jan 2025, only ~97,000 (1.4%) kept ≥$1,000 of liquidity. Solidus calls the rest "98.6% rug pulls or pump-and-dumps" ([coverage](https://forklog.com/en/report-98-of-pump-funs-memecoins-deemed-scams/)). · **med**

### Supporting detail
- **2026 is a counterpoint an investor may raise.** CoinGecko found the profitable share rose to 56.8% (Feb), 70.0% (Mar) and 73.3% (Apr 2026), its best in two years. But the largest profit bucket was $1–$500 (~2.05M wallets), and monthly active wallets had fallen 65%, from 5.2M (May 2025) to 1.8M (Dec 2025). CoinGecko suggests unprofitable traders left.
- **Fresher but rougher 2026 data:** a Dune dashboard by @oladee covers ~1.4M wallets. In March 2026, 49–50.6% of them lost money, ~96% "lost or made under $500", and 2 wallets made >$1M ([crypto.news, Mar 2026](https://crypto.news/pump-fun-data-shows-49-of-march-traders-in-the-red-as-platform-locks-fees/)). · **med-low** (pseudonymous, month-to-date, and it conflicts with CoinGecko's 70%)
- **Graduation since BOOST:** from late July 2026, daily graduation rates have been 2.5–6.7% (The Block).
- **Aggregate retail losses:** I found no neutral, measured total. Unverified figures from memory (confirm before use): the pump.fun class action alleges ~$5.5B in losses, Nansen found ~86% of LIBRA traders lost money, and Chainalysis found ~813K wallets lost money on $TRUMP. I did not reach academic papers.

### Suggested slide copy
1. "9 in 10 pump.fun traders lost money or made under $100."
2. "13.5 million wallets. 293 millionaires."
3. "97% of pump.fun traders never made $1,000."

Spoken: "On pump.fun, roughly nine in ten traders lost money or cleared less than a hundred dollars. Of 13.5 million wallets, fewer than 300 ever banked a million."

### Caveats / what not to claim
- **Do not say "9 in 10 lose money."** Depending on the period, 50–70% lost money outright. "9 in 10" holds only with "or made under $100" and a 2024 date.
- **Don't present 2024 data as current; date every footnote.** If challenged: "even in its best month in two years, the biggest group of winners made under $500."
- **Realized-PnL studies undercount losers.** Wallets still holding dead tokens are excluded (CoinGecko says so explicitly). Wallets ≠ people, and bots inflate the counts. The Jan 2025 data ignores trades made after a token graduated.
- **The ">$1,000" figure differs between sources** (3% vs 0.76%). Use "fewer than 3%" or "97%+".
- **The 56.6% bucket is losses of $0–$1K,** not "at least $1K" as some coverage misstates.
- **"Rug pull" is Solidus's label.** What was measured is liquidity collapsing below $1,000.
- **Attribution:** the 3%/60% breakdown is from Aug 2024, not late 2024. I couldn't confirm it came from adam_tehc's dashboard. Decrypt credits Adam Tehc only for the Jan 2025 data.
