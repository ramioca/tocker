# 28 · Retail memecoin trader outcomes, 2025–2026 (for the problem slide)

## On the slide: 94% made no profit (90 days to Aug 2026)
- **Figure:** 285,158 of 304,161 Solana memecoin wallets (93.75%) did not finish in profit over 90 days. 19,003 (6.25%) did.
  - The median result was a $120 loss.
  - 88% of profitable wallets made under $100. Only 25 wallets made more than $10k realized.
- **Data:** wallet-level Dune data on fomo-app memecoin traders. Attribution varies by outlet: "FOMO and Dune Analytics", or "active on Solana DEX".
  - Measured on realized PnL / net cash flow, so unsold tokens are not valued.
  - Earliest report found: cryptonews.ru, 2026-08-19.
  - Sources: [Cryptopolitan](https://www.cryptopolitan.com/6-solana-meme-traders-profit-in-90-days/), [Cointribune](https://www.cointribune.com/en/crypto-solana-memecoins-trap-nearly-94-of-traders), [HTX](https://www.htx.com/news/what-are-the-odds-only-6-of-solana-meme-traders-made-a-profi-5gTkabXy/), [DailyCoin](https://dailycoin.com/only-25-wallets-cleared-10k-as-solana-meme-coins-surge).
- **Wording:** say "made no profit", not "lost money". The 93.75% includes break-even wallets.
  - An independent re-run of a similar fomo query (Kliment Dukovski, Sep 2 2026) found 5.69% of 476,627 wallets net positive. He cautions that this is 90-day SOL/stable cash flow, not full PnL ([source](https://klimentdukovski.com/articles/fomo-app-review/)).
  - The ~$1.26B aggregate-loss figure in some write-ups is the least certain. Don't use it.

## Alternatives
- **Pump.fun, March 2026** (Dune dashboard by @oladee, ~1.4M wallets):
  - 50.6% lost money.
  - 45.4–45.6% made under $500.
  - So ~96% lost money or made under $500.
  - Often misreported as "96% lost money".
  - Covered 24–26 Mar 2026, possibly a partial month ([MEXC](https://www.mexc.com/news/992440), [DailyCoin](https://dailycoin.com/pump-funs-harsh-truth-most-users-face-losses/)).
- **Pump.fun, Jun 2025** (Dune, 4.26M addresses with more than 10 tokens traded in 6 months):
  - more than 60% lost money;
  - 56.6% lost $0–$1,000 ([BeInCrypto](https://beincrypto.com/pump-fun-trading-data-majority-lose-money/)).
- **Earlier, replaced on the slide:** "9 in 10 pump.fun traders lost money or made under $100 in 2024" (Dune wallet PnL via The Defiant, Aug 2024).
- **Avoid:** the viral "97% of pump.fun traders lose money" (early 2026, via aixbt). It has no visible method.

## Caveats for Q&A
- Wallets are not people: bots, insiders and multi-wallet users distort counts.
- None of these is peer-reviewed. They come from public Dune dashboards relayed by the press.
