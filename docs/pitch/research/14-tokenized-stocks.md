## The "81%" stat: confirmed, but not about stocks
On 2 Sep 2026 trading terminals did **$1.03B**, their first day above $1B since the TRUMP launch. **Robinhood Chain had 81.2% ($834.7M)** and Solana had 14.5% ($149.4M). GMGN did $479.7M and fomo $268.2M. Sources: [CoinTurk](https://en.coin-turk.com/trading-terminal-daily-volume-hits-1-03-billion-robinhood-chain-leads-with-81yuzde-share/), The Defiant, Galaxy Research.

The volume was driven by **memecoins**, not equities. The drivers were a 90-day gas subsidy funded by Robinhood, EVM bots working on day one, the Pons launchpad (about 646K tokens minted), and memecoins paired directly against stock-token liquidity. **Don't use it as a tokenized-stock stat.** It does show that a stock-token chain can pull in speculative bot flow within weeks.
