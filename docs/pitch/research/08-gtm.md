## Go-to-market: every fill is distribution

### Evidence from comparables
- **fomo (closest analog):** verified-PnL trade feed, leaderboards, followers. 120K users at its Nov-2025 Series A [1], 1.9M by 31 Aug 2026; daily traders grew from <10K to 80–100K+ (Jun–Aug 2026). Engines: a 25% referral fee-share, "Trader Rewards" for posts that move others' trades, Clans team leaderboards [2]. Top 5 in US iPhone Finance [3].
- **Axiom (YC W25):** $100M revenue about 4 months after launch [5]. 2% → 72% of Solana bot volume (Feb → mid-2025) on referral codes, volume-based SOL rebates and perps [4]. fomo later overtook it [19].
- **Photon / BullX / GMGN:** 30–35% lifetime referral shares [6, secondary]; GMGN also bought Telegram ads [7, secondary]. Axiom still took share: referral alone is no moat [4].
- **Alpha Arena (Nof1):** six frontier models, $10K of real money each, 17 days [8]. "Over 50M views on social media" (company-reported) [9].
- **Recall:** ~1M users across its first nine agent-trading events (self-reported) [10].
- **Kolscan:** KOL PnL leaderboard Pump.fun bought as a social/gamified growth tool [11]; a petition says it lets KOLs farm copy-traders [12].
- **Hyperliquid:** weekly points with an affiliate share [13], then 31% of supply to ~94K addresses [14].
- **Polymarket:** odds spread as X content. 314.5K monthly traders in Dec 2024 [15]; X's official partner from Jun 2025 [16].
- **Pump.fun:** creator-fee model paid ~$2M in 24h [17].

### Recommended tactics
1. **Fill cards + leaderboard as the core loop** (fomo [1–2], Kolscan [11]). Render every fill as a share card: token, score, PnL, rationale, "verified on Tocker".
2. **Seasons: agent vs agent, creator vs creator** (Alpha Arena [9], Recall [10]). Open with frontier models running identical agents. Alpha Arena's hook was real money: separate paper and live boards; rank risk-adjusted.
3. **Creator agents: "proof without the recipe"** (referrals [2][4]; Kolscan backlash [12]). KOLs get a verified record nobody can copy-trade, plus a fee-share.
4. **Paper → live funnel:** the weakest evidence. Nobody publishes conversion, though Recall shows no-capital contests draw crowds [10].

**"Largest books first" is a weak fit.** With a flat $0.10 fee, revenue tracks fills, not book size; distribution tracks audience. Visible whale books also draw stop-hunters (Hyperliquid's James Wynn [18]). Rank the waitlist by expected fills × audience.

### Phased plan
- **0–3 mo, Season 0 (closed):** onboard [N] creators + [N] active books; ship share cards, the 7d board and referral codes; run the model exhibition. KPIs: waitlist→agent activation %, weekly active agents, fills/day, card shares/CTR, D7 operator retention.
- **3–9 mo, public seasons:** a paper league with [prize pool], creator brackets, clans, fee-share, Telegram alerts. KPIs: season entrants, paper→live conversion %, live agents, referred-signup share, K-factor.
- **9–18 mo, scale:** sponsored seasons, Base creators, a public agent API. KPIs: monthly fills (revenue ÷ $0.10), live capital, M3 live retention, CAC payback.

**Channels:** X first (cards and recaps; where Alpha Arena and Polymarket spread [9][16]); Telegram for alerts; Discord for season ops; arena livestreams [17].
**Wedge:** Solana new-launch traders and the KOLs already ranked on PnL trackers. They have audiences, hate copy-traders and trade often. Base second.

### Suggested slide copy
1. **"Every fill is a post. Every post is distribution."** (recommended)
2. "Public record, private recipe: why creators bring their audience."
3. "Agent seasons: Alpha Arena for everyone, free on paper."

Spoken: "fomo grew from 120 thousand to 1.9 million users in under ten months on a feed of verified trades. Tocker makes every agent fill a verified post, runs free paper seasons, and gives creators a public record that never leaks their edge."

### Caveats / what not to claim
- Tocker has a waitlist, not traction.
- No tokens or airdrops. Use fee credits or prizes; contests need legal review.
- Never say "copy-trade".
- A creator's cut of $0.10 is cents, so sell reputation, not income.
- Page fetches were blocked: figures come from search excerpts, mostly company-reported. Spot-check.
