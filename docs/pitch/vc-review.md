# Tocker deck: Series A partner review

## 1. Slide by slide
- **Cover (5s: half).** "Infrastructure" sets you up against Coinbase Agentic Wallets and Privy. What you've built is a consumer app.
- **Problem (5s: yes).** The stats tell a speed story (insiders in 0.4s), but Tocker waits until a token is 30 minutes old by default. Your real pain point is screening and discipline. "9 in 10" is Aug 2024 data, and by Apr 2026 CoinGecko had 73% of wallets profitable. "Only funds can" is false, because Axiom and Photon serve retail at 1%.
- **Solution (5s: yes).** It lists features without saying why an LLM agent makes money. In Alpha Arena, 4 of 6 frontier models lost 31–63%.
- **Product (5s: yes).** This is the strongest slide, but it's a mock-up. It needs a real record of paper or live trades.
- **Alpha (5s: mostly).** It suggests a honeypot check everywhere, but Plexa covers Base only, and Nansen and Plexa are off by default. It also doesn't say that **Tocker pays** for this data.
- **Business (5s: yes, and wrong).** "20 bps" contradicts the code (`DEFAULT_PLATFORM_FEE_USD = 0.1`, a flat fee per fill). The $1.7B includes $762M of launchpad fees and is a 2025 peak. Q2 2026 ran at 43% of that pace.
- **Competition (not credible).** The grid is self-graded, and "x402 data" is a feature you chose, not a buyer need. Ask Gina (backed by Coinbase Ventures; Recipes that run 24/7; Privy wallet; sponsored gas) is nearly the same product. fomo was valued at $550M. Fere AI and Bankr are missing.
- **GTM (5s: yes).** "Verified PnL" sits next to demo paper data. fomo is human social trading, so it doesn't prove agents spread the same way. A creator's cut of a dime is pennies.
- **Thanks (wasted).** There's no team, traction, ask or why-now.

## 2. The five hardest questions
1. **"Traction?"** Honest answer: a waitlist, private beta, paper by default, live trading tested only by the founder, and 269 commits in about 3 weeks.
2. **"Unit economics?"** A typical default agent burns about $8.80 a day in data and earns at most about $2 a day in fees. Answer with a dated plan: a shared per-token cache, hourly cadence, and data paid from the agent's wallet.
3. **"Why would an LLM win when Alpha Arena's didn't?"** Don't claim alpha. Sell discipline: 10 hard gates, exits enforced in code, and a kill switch that never blocks an exit. Retail mostly loses to rugs and to holding too long.
4. **"What stops Coinbase or Ask Gina?"** Wallets and x402 are commodity rails. Your edges are scoring of new launches, a public record that keeps the strategy private (it can't be copy-traded), and network effects from the leaderboard. Admit it's a race.
5. **"Custody and regulation?"** Cover Privy's delegated keys, whether this counts as investment advice, retail losing money on memecoins, and Polymarket and tokenized stocks. Say plainly: no token, no performance fee.

## 3. Top five changes
1. **Turn "Thanks" into team, traction and the ask:** the amount, 18-month milestones (live agents, fills a day, paper-to-live %), and the waitlist number.
2. **Fix the business slide:** use the real fee, "$940M paid to Solana trading bots in 2025", one line on data cost per agent, and the path to positive margin.
3. **Rebuild the problem slide around losing to rugs and holding too long:** "98.6% of pump.fun tokens collapse" (Solidus) and a dated "most lose". Drop the 0.4s line and "only funds".
4. **Turn competition into a 2×2:** autonomous vs manual, and screened new launches vs generic. Swap the x402 column for "Strategy stays private".
5. **Put why-now on the solution slide:** the x402 Foundation (Visa, Stripe, AWS, Apr 2026), cheap models, and agent wallets reaching GA. Recast the cover as a consumer agent, not infrastructure.

## 4. Unsupported or risky claims
- **"20 bps, 5× cheaper":** contradicts the code. Fatal in diligence.
- **"$1.7B … just for memecoin trading tools":** includes launchpads and uses 2025 peak numbers.
- **"9 in 10":** 2024 data, wallets rather than people, and contradicted by 2026 data.
- **"0.4s (Pine Analytics)":** 0.4s is Solana's slot time. Pine measured sniping in the first block.
- **"A failed sell check blocks the buy":** true only on Base.
- **Gas plus all data paid by Tocker against a ~$2/day fee ceiling:** negative margin as built.
